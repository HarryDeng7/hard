        Check in once per day. 7 days in a row unlocks the Golden Legend avatar frame — and every check-in pays +2 coins for the claw machine.

        📖 How the check-in works

        1. Once a day. Every day you open the arcade, hit Check in. One check-in per day — the button locks itself once you're done.
        2. Streaks build up. Each check-in grows your streak (🔥). Miss a day and the streak resets to 1 — no mercy, just like a real arcade.
        3. Coins every day. Every check-in pays +2 coins for the claw machine, so the machine never runs dry.
        4. The golden prize. Check in 7 days in a row and the Golden Legend avatar frame is yours — confetti included.
        5. Frames are forever. Once unlocked, the frame stays in your locker. Equip or swap it anytime in the Avatar frames section below.

        The 7 circles above are your last 7 days. Streaks are per player — every account races for its own golden frame.
